FIELD GUIDE

Goal
Trade shots until one dragon falls. Campaign: climb stages, earn EXP and gold, level up, buy gear. 2-Player Duel: two dragons, one device — pass and play.
Your turn
Move with ◀ ▶ (or A/D) — drains stamina, refilled next turn.
Aim with ＋/− (or W/S). Fire by holding FIRE (or Space) to charge power, release to launch — or drag back from your dragon like a slingshot.
Firing, healing, shielding, or blinking ends your turn. Potions don't.
Your previous shot's angle, power and wind stay on screen, and a gold ✕ marks where it landed — read the new wind and correct from your last shot, artillery style. A gold line on FIRE marks your last power.
Wind, terrain & obstacles
The pennant shows the wind — it bends every shot and changes each turn. The whole battlefield is on screen by default; tap 🎯 (or press Z) for a close-up action camera that follows movement and shots. Blasts carve craters; dragons take fall damage. Stone spires block your path and floating rocks block your shots — arc over them or blast them apart. Wooden supply crates sometimes sit on the field — break one for an instant gold payout. Battles rage over meadow, cinder wastes (ember rain occasionally chips HP off obstacles and crates), frozen reaches (gusts periodically double the wind), or a sundered chasm that splits the field — arc your shots over the gap, or risk a fall. The chasm's own gap widens with periodic tremors, so its footing only gets tighter as the fight goes on.
Skills & MP
Skills cost MP (+14 regained per turn). Every dragon shares Flame Shot, Twin Shot, Mega Blast, Heal, Shield, and 🪞 Ward (raises a ward that reflects a share of the next hit back at its attacker), plus two signature skills — the second unlocks at level 4 and a third at level 8: life-stealing boomerangs, freezing slabs, splitting meteors, sky bolts, bouncing stones, poison clouds, walls of ice, a shadow-step escape, and more. Dusk alone grows a 4th signature at level 10: 🖤 Night Ward, a deeper ward that reflects more of the hit and drains life from it. Spend level-up skill points in the Den's Skills panel to train any of them stronger.
Gear & stats
The shop sells five permanent gear lines: ATK raises damage · DEF lowers damage taken · AGI extends stamina · LUK raises crit chance · 🔰 Aegis Ward softens hits from a foe holding the elemental advantage. See it all at a glance in the Den's loadout row.
Amplifiers
Shop-bought one-shot items that bend a single turn, just like potions — carry up to 2 of each: 🍃 Calm Wind zeroes the wind for this turn's shot, 💥 Overcharge adds +30% damage to your next shot, 🔭 Scope reveals the exact wind for your next two turns. None of them end your turn.
Magic stones
Victories sometimes drop a stone carrying the defeated dragon's element — alphas always drop one, and drop better tiers. Socket up to 3 in the Den's Stones panel for a % ATK bonus, full value if it matches your own dragon's element. Three of the same tier and element synthesize into one of the next tier.
Elements
Every dragon carries an element — Fire, Ice, Thunder, Earth, Shadow, or Toxin — set on a wheel where each is strong against one and weak against another (a ▲/▼ badge on the HUD plate, an "Effective!"/"Resisted" float on hit). Nyx, a 7th dragon earned by felling 3 alpha bosses, carries the Void element and sits outside the wheel entirely — neutral against every matchup.
Alpha bosses
Every 5th stage is an alpha — a named boss with a tougher fight. Past 40% HP it enrages, hitting harder and fighting more aggressively, and triggers its own signature hazard: Cindermaw scorches the ground, Glacierfang raises an ice wall, Quakehide cracks open a crater, Stormcrown's bolt strikes a second point, Nightgorge blinks behind you, Plaguewing's cloud lingers. Fought down past 15% HP it enrages a second time (💢), hitting and aiming even more dangerously. Felling one always pays a bonus skill point.
Beyond the ladder
Every win is graded S/A/B/C by how fast and clean the hunt was, for an EXP/gold bonus. From the Den you can also launch a Side Hunt (an off-ladder practice fight for reduced rewards), a Trial (an off-ladder fight with a constraint — No Healing, Windstorm, or Halved Stamina — that pays better than a Side Hunt), or Spar against a leveled AI partner — a fully no-stakes fight that pays nothing and never touches your stage, gold, EXP, or record, for trying a new loadout risk-free. Milestones quietly pay out as Achievements, tracked in their own Den panel. Once you've reached stage 10, New Game+ resets your ladder run for a small permanent stat bonus plus extra starting gold each time, keeping your career record intact.
